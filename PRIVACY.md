# Privacy Policy

**Workout Tracker**
Effective date: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Workout Tracker app for iPhone and iPad ("the App") handles your information. In short, **the App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.**

## Information We Collect

We do not collect any information. The App has no user accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It makes no network requests to us or to any third party. The only network use is Apple's iCloud sync described below, which goes to your own iCloud account.

## Information Stored on Your Device

To work, the App saves the following data **only on your device**:

- Workout templates, exercises and default weights and reps
- Workout sessions, completed sets, dates and times
- Profile details you choose to enter: first name, last name and an optional profile photo
- App preferences such as appearance (light/dark) and language

Apart from the optional iCloud sync and Apple Health features below, this data does not leave your device through the App. We cannot access it.

The profile photo is picked with the system photo picker. The App receives only the photo you select, not your photo library.

## iCloud Sync

If you are signed in to iCloud and "Sync with iCloud" is on in the App's Profile tab (on by default), your workout templates and workout history are stored in the private CloudKit database of **your own iCloud account** so they appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. This data is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy. We, the developer, have no access to your private iCloud database. Profile details and preferences are not synced. You can turn sync off at any time in the Profile tab.

## Apple Health

If you turn on "Save Workouts to Health" in the Profile tab, the App asks for permission to **write** workouts to the Apple Health app. When you finish a workout, the App saves it as a strength training workout with its start and end time. The App never reads any Health data, and Health data is never used for advertising or shared with anyone. You can revoke access in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices.

## Backup Files

The App can export your data to a JSON backup file and restore it. The file is created only when you choose to export it, and you decide where it goes (for example Files, iCloud Drive or AirDrop). The App does not send it anywhere on its own.

## Backups

If you have iCloud Backup or computer backups turned on, your device may include the App's data in those backups. Apple manages this under its own privacy policy and your device settings, not the App.

## Third-Party Services

The App uses no third-party SDKs, analytics tools or advertising networks.

## Deleting Your Data

You can delete workouts and templates inside the App at any time. To remove all App data, delete the App from your device.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
